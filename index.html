01 / THE APPROACH

See more clearly.
Assume less.

VISIONSHIELD is designed around a simple idea: no single sensor should be trusted blindly. Independent signals are evaluated together, with visibility and persistence shaping how much confidence an event deserves.

01

Multimodal by design

RGB and low-resolution thermal evidence complement one another when lighting, weather, or the scene itself is difficult.

02

Local by default

The proposed edge architecture keeps sensing and first-stage decisions close to the device, reducing unnecessary data movement.

03

Evidence over certainty

Persistent, multi-signal evidence should be required before an event is confirmed. Unknown stays unknown until validated.
